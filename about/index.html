A LITTLE CONTEXT

Hi, I'm Roberto.

I'm a solutions architect working at the intersection of cloud infrastructure, networking, and emerging AI technologies.

My work is centred on helping organisations turn complex technical ideas into systems that can operate in the real world. Lately, much of my attention has been on agentic AI: the architecture, integration patterns, security foundations, and engineering practices required to move beyond prototypes.

This site is where I collect ideas, technical notes, and perspectives shaped by that work. I write about the systems behind the technology as much as the technology itself.

If you're exploring similar questions or working on something interesting, I'd be glad to hear from you.
